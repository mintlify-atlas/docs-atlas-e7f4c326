A consistent study schedule is the single biggest predictor of exam success. Whether you have four weeks or three months, the key is distributing study time across all four domains in proportion to their exam weight. The schedules below are built directly from the CLF-C02 domain weightings — Domain 3 (34%) and Domain 2 (30%) receive the most time, while Domain 4 (12%) gets a focused but shorter sprint. Pick the timeline that fits your life, then stick to it.

<Note>
  Adjust any schedule based on your prior cloud experience. Coming in with zero cloud background? Add **20% more time per domain** — especially for Domain 3 (Technology & Services), which covers the most service-specific detail.
</Note>

## Choose Your Study Timeline

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="4-Week Intensive">
    **Best for:** Dedicated learners who can commit 2.5–3.5 hours per day. Total: ~77 hours.

    ### Week 1 — Cloud Concepts & Security Foundation (17.5 hrs)
    | Days | Focus | Topics |
    |------|-------|--------|
    | Days 1–3 | Cloud Concepts core | What is cloud computing, the six advantages of cloud, public/private/hybrid deployment models |
    | Days 4–5 | Service models + infrastructure | IaaS vs PaaS vs SaaS with AWS examples, AWS Regions, Availability Zones, Edge Locations |
    | Days 6–7 | Security introduction | Shared Responsibility Model basics, intro to IAM Users and Groups |

    ### Week 2 — Security & Compliance Deep Dive (21 hrs)
    | Days | Focus | Topics |
    |------|-------|--------|
    | Days 1–2 | IAM deep dive | Users, Groups, Roles, Policies, Principle of Least Privilege, when to use Roles vs Users |
    | Days 3–4 | Security services | MFA, GuardDuty, Macie, Inspector, Shield, WAF, CloudTrail, AWS Config |
    | Days 5–6 | Compliance | AWS Artifact, PCI DSS, HIPAA, SOC 2, compliance frameworks overview |
    | Day 7 | Review + quiz | Re-read your Domain 2 notes, complete 15 practice questions on security |

    ### Week 3 — Technology & Services (24.5 hrs)
    | Days | Focus | Topics |
    |------|-------|--------|
    | Days 1–2 | Compute | EC2 instance types, pricing options (O-R-S-S-D), Lambda, containers (ECS, EKS, Fargate) |
    | Days 3–4 | Storage | S3 storage classes, EBS vs EFS vs S3 comparison, AWS Snow Family |
    | Day 5 | Networking | VPC, subnets, security groups, NACLs, internet gateway, CloudFront, Route 53 |
    | Day 6 | Databases | RDS, DynamoDB, ElastiCache, Redshift — when to use each |
    | Day 7 | Additional services | SQS, SNS, CloudWatch, AI/ML services (Rekognition, Comprehend, Translate, Lex, SageMaker) |

    ### Week 4 — Billing, Practice Exams & Review (14 hrs)
    | Days | Focus | Topics |
    |------|-------|--------|
    | Days 1–2 | Billing + pricing | Pricing models, Free Tier tiers, Cost Explorer, Budgets, Cost and Usage Report |
    | Day 3 | Support plans | All four plans (Basic, Developer, Business, Enterprise), TAM, 24/7 phone access threshold |
    | Days 4–5 | Practice Exams 1 & 2 | Take each exam under timed conditions; review every incorrect answer |
    | Days 6–7 | Weak areas + final exam | Targeted review of lowest-scoring domains, Practice Exam 3, then rest |

    <Tip>
      On days when life gets busy, protect at least **one 25-minute Pomodoro session** over nothing. A shorter session keeps momentum; skipping entirely breaks the habit.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="8-Week Balanced">
    **Best for:** Working professionals with 1.5–2 hours per day. Total: ~87.5 hours.

    ### Weeks 1–2 — Cloud Concepts (1.5 hrs/day)
    Build your cloud foundation before touching any AWS-specific services.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Week 1">
        What is cloud computing, on-premises vs cloud tradeoffs, the six advantages of cloud. Practice defining each benefit in your own words.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Week 2">
        Deployment models (public, private, hybrid), service models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS) with concrete AWS examples, AWS global infrastructure (Regions, AZs, Edge Locations). Complete 10 Domain 1 practice questions.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ### Weeks 3–4 — Security & Compliance (1.5 hrs/day)
    The highest-weighted conceptual domain (30%) — give it full attention.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Week 3">
        Shared Responsibility Model scenarios for EC2, RDS, and Lambda. IAM deep dive: Users, Groups, Roles, Policies, Principle of Least Privilege, MFA requirements.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Week 4">
        Security services survey (GuardDuty, Macie, Inspector, Shield, WAF, CloudTrail, AWS Config). Compliance programs and how to access compliance documents via AWS Artifact. Complete 15 Domain 2 practice questions.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ### Weeks 5–6 — Technology & Services (2 hrs/day)
    The largest domain (34%) — allocate extra daily time here.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Week 5">
        Compute: EC2 pricing options, Lambda serverless model, containers (ECS/EKS/Fargate), choosing the right compute service for a scenario. Storage: S3 storage classes and lifecycle policies, EBS vs EFS vs S3, Snow Family.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Week 6">
        Networking: VPC architecture, subnets, security groups vs NACLs, CloudFront CDN, Route 53 routing policies. Databases: RDS vs DynamoDB decision criteria, ElastiCache use case, Redshift. Additional services: SQS, SNS, CloudWatch vs CloudTrail, five key AI/ML services.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ### Week 7 — Billing & Pricing (1.5 hrs/day)
    Smaller domain (12%) but very testable — know the support plans cold.

    - On-Demand, Reserved, Spot, Savings Plans pricing models
    - AWS Free Tier: always-free vs 12-months free vs trials
    - Cost Explorer, Budgets, Cost and Usage Report
    - AWS Organizations and consolidated billing
    - All four support plans: Basic, Developer, Business (24/7 phone), Enterprise (TAM)

    ### Week 8 — Review & Practice Exams (1 hr/day + exam blocks)
    | Activity | Time |
    |----------|------|
    | Practice Exam 1 (timed) | 90 minutes |
    | Review Exam 1 incorrect answers | 60 minutes |
    | Practice Exam 2 (timed) | 90 minutes |
    | Review Exam 2 incorrect answers | 60 minutes |
    | Targeted weak-area review | 2–3 sessions |
    | Practice Exam 3 + cheat sheet review | 90 minutes + 30 minutes |
    | Rest day before exam | — |

    <Tip>
      Don't cram on the final day. A rested brain recalls information more accurately than an exhausted one. Trust your preparation.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="12-Week Relaxed">
    **Best for:** Beginners or learners with limited daily availability (~1 hr/day). Total: ~85 hours.

    ### Month 1, Weeks 1–3 — Cloud Concepts (6 hrs/week)
    Take the time to truly understand why cloud computing exists and what problems it solves. Don't rush to AWS-specific services yet.

    | Week | Focus |
    |------|-------|
    | Week 1 | What is cloud, on-premises vs cloud cost models, the six advantages in depth |
    | Week 2 | Deployment models, service models — build analogies that make sense to you |
    | Week 3 | AWS global infrastructure, Regions vs AZs vs Edge Locations, quiz yourself on Domain 1 |

    ### Months 1–2, Weeks 4–6 — Security & Compliance (7 hrs/week)
    Security is the second-heaviest domain — spend extra time making the Shared Responsibility Model intuitive.

    | Week | Focus |
    |------|-------|
    | Week 4 | Shared Responsibility Model: draw the line for EC2, RDS, Lambda, and S3 |
    | Week 5 | IAM: Users, Groups, Roles, Policies, MFA, Principle of Least Privilege |
    | Week 6 | Security services (GuardDuty through WAF), compliance frameworks, AWS Artifact |

    ### Months 2–3, Weeks 7–9 — Technology & Services (8 hrs/week)
    The heaviest domain — give yourself three full weeks and don't try to cover everything in one sitting.

    | Week | Focus |
    |------|-------|
    | Week 7 | Compute: EC2 (types, pricing), Lambda, containers (ECS/EKS/Fargate) |
    | Week 8 | Storage: S3 classes, EBS vs EFS vs S3, Snow Family. Networking: VPC, CloudFront, Route 53 |
    | Week 9 | Databases: RDS, DynamoDB, ElastiCache. Additional: SQS, SNS, CloudWatch, AI/ML services |

    ### Month 3, Weeks 10–11 — Billing & Review (5 hrs/week)
    | Week | Focus |
    |------|-------|
    | Week 10 | Pricing models, Free Tier tiers, Cost Explorer, Budgets, Organizations |
    | Week 11 | Support plans, consolidated billing, full review of all four domains with cheat sheet |

    ### Week 12 — Practice Exams & Final Prep (4 hrs total)
    | Activity | Time |
    |----------|------|
    | Practice Exam 1 + review | 2.5 hours |
    | Practice Exam 2 + review | 2.5 hours |
    | Practice Exam 3 + cheat sheet scan | 2 hours |
    | Rest + exam day prep | Final evening |

    <Note>
      If you're scoring below 75% after Practice Exam 2, take one additional week to review your weakest domain before scheduling your real exam. There is no shame in being thorough.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Daily Study Session Tips

No matter which schedule you choose, these session-level habits make every hour more effective.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Use the Pomodoro Technique" icon="stopwatch">
    Work in **25-minute focused blocks**, then take a **5-minute break**. After four blocks, take a 20-minute break. This prevents mental fatigue and keeps retention high throughout longer sessions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Start With a 5-Minute Recap" icon="rotate-left">
    Spend the first 5 minutes of every session reviewing what you covered the day before. Spaced retrieval — not re-reading — is what moves facts into long-term memory.
  </Card>
  <Card title="End With Practice Questions" icon="circle-question">
    Close every study session with **3–5 practice questions** on the material you just covered. Immediate application reveals gaps while the content is still fresh.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Track Your Completion" icon="list-check">
    Use the [Progress Checklist](/final-prep/progress-checklist) to mark off topics as you master them. Visible progress reduces anxiety and keeps you motivated through the longer schedules.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Don't skip breaks — spaced repetition and active recall work far better than marathon sessions. Two focused hours beat six distracted hours every time.
</Tip>
