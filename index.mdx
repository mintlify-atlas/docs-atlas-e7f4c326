## Exam Domains

The CLF-C02 exam covers four domains. Study them in order or jump to the area where you need the most work.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Domain 1: Cloud Concepts" icon="cloud" href="/cloud-concepts/what-is-cloud">
    **24% of the exam.** What cloud computing is, its benefits, deployment models, service models, and AWS global infrastructure.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Domain 2: Security & Compliance" icon="shield-halved" href="/security-compliance/shared-responsibility">
    **30% of the exam.** Shared responsibility model, IAM, security services, and AWS compliance programs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Domain 3: Technology & Services" icon="server" href="/technology-services/compute-services">
    **34% of the exam.** Core AWS services: compute, storage, networking, databases, and more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Domain 4: Billing & Support" icon="credit-card" href="/billing-support/pricing-models">
    **12% of the exam.** Pricing models, cost management tools, and AWS support plans.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Use This Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with the Exam Overview">
    Review the [Exam Overview](/exam-overview) to understand the CLF-C02 structure, domain weights, and passing score before you dive into content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Study Schedule">
    Visit [Study Plans](/study-plans) to choose a 4-week intensive, 8-week balanced, or 12-week relaxed schedule based on your available time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work Through All Four Domains">
    Study each domain in order — Cloud Concepts → Security & Compliance → Technology & Services → Billing & Support — following your chosen schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test Yourself with Practice Exams">
    Once you've covered all domains, take all three [Practice Exams](/practice-exams/practice-exam-1) and review your weak areas using the Quick Reference materials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Final Review">
    Use the [Final Preparation](/final-prep/overview) section for a 7-day review checklist, day-of-exam strategies, and a progress tracker before your test date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This guide targets the **CLF-C02** version of the AWS Cloud Practitioner exam (updated 2023). All content is aligned to the official AWS exam guide objectives.
</Note>
